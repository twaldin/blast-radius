Slack
Applicable to the services branded as (a) Slack, including Slack AI and (b) GovSlack.

Purpose of Processing | Sub-processors | Locations | Additional Details

ROW 1
Purpose: Slack services, including Slack AI (excluding GovSlack): Hosting provider for all functionality. Customers may select a data region, but as described in this help article, certain Customer Data may be stored outside this data region.
Sub-processor: Amazon Web Services, Inc.
Locations: United States, Australia, Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, India, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Sweden, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom
Additional Details: Customer Data from the Germany region is backed up in France, and vice versa. Customer Data from the Sweden region is backed up in Germany. Customer Data from the Switzerland region is backed up in Germany.

ROW 2
Purpose: Slack services including Slack AI (excluding GovSlack): Hosting provider for Availability zones/Edge servers for all functionality.
Sub-processor: Amazon Web Services, Inc.
Locations: United States, Australia, Germany, India, Ireland, Japan, Singapore, South Africa, United Kingdom, Brazil, Sweden
Additional Details: Customer Data may be transmitted through any Edge region to provide better and faster service response time, depending, for example, on the location of Slack Users.

ROW 3
Purpose: GovSlack services: Hosting provider for all functionality.
Sub-processor: Amazon Web Services, Inc.
Locations: United States
Additional Details: Customer Data is stored on the AWS US-GOV-EAST instance. Salesforce may route the transmission of encrypted Customer Data through various points, including any of its US AWS GovCloud data centers, but the storage of Customer Data will be limited to the US-GOV-EAST data center.

ROW 4
Purpose: GovSlack services: Hosting provider for Availability zones/Edge servers for all functionality
Sub-processor: Amazon Web Services, Inc.
Locations: United States
Additional Details: Customer Data may be transmitted through any AWS GovCloud Edge region to provide better and faster service response time depending, for example, on the location of Slack Users.

ROW 5
Purpose: Slack Huddles functionality (excluding GovSlack): audio and video streams.
Sub-processor: Amazon Chime (by Amazon Web Services, Inc.)
Locations: United States, Australia, Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, India, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Sweden, United Kingdom, Switzerland, Germany, United Arab Emirates

ROW 6
Purpose: Slack Clips functionality (excluding GovSlack): conversion of source video for improved playback
Sub-processor: Amazon Web Services, Inc. Elemental MediaConvert
Locations: United States, Australia, Canada, France, Germany, India, Japan, South Korea, United Kingdom, Brazil, Sweden, Switzerland

ROW 7
Purpose: Slack Huddles and Slack Clips functionality (excluding GovSlack): transcripts for Slack Huddles and Slack Clips
Sub-processor: Amazon Transcribe (by Amazon Web Services, Inc.)
Locations: United States, Australia, Canada, France, Germany, India, Japan, South Korea, United Kingdom, Brazil, Sweden, Switzerland

ROW 8
Purpose: Slack Huddles functionality (GovSlack): audio and video streams.
Sub-processor: Amazon Chime (by Amazon Web Services, Inc.)
Locations: United States

ROW 9
Purpose: Slack Clips functionality (GovSlack): conversion of source video for improved playback
Sub-processor: Amazon Web Services, Inc. Elemental MediaConvert
Locations: United States

ROW 10
Purpose: Slack Huddles and Slack Clips functionality (GovSlack): transcripts for Slack Huddles and Slack Clips
Sub-processor: Amazon Transcribe (by Amazon Web Services, Inc.)
Locations: United States

ROW 11 (single table row in the PDF; the purpose cell lists both purposes and the sub-processor cell lists Google LLC once)
Purpose: Hosting Provider for email notifications (e.g., workspace invitations). Provider of generative artificial intelligence services.
Sub-processor: Google LLC
Locations: United States

ROW 12
Purpose: CDN for all functionality
Sub-processor: Cloudfront (on Amazon Web Services, Inc.)
Locations: Global

ROW 13
Purpose: Service provider of customer support tools
Sub-processor: Zendesk, Inc.
Locations: United States

ROW 14
Purpose: Provider of generative artificial intelligence services.
Sub-processor: Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Azure)
Locations: United States

ROW 15
Purpose: Provider of web search results for Slackbot.
Sub-processor: SuSea, Inc. (doing business as You.com)
Locations: United States
Additional Details: If enabled by Customer, SuSea, Inc. may transmit public website links and content retrieved by prompts or queries to Slackbot in order to include such links and content in generative output.
