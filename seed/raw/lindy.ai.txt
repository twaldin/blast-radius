Additional details

Lindy's mission is to free humanity from work, by building a no-code platform for AI/LLM agents. We believe that while much of the recent AI developments around LLMs are focused on generative aspects (image, video, and prose generation), there is a large untapped opportunity in using AI to automate employee workloads.

Privacy details

We have designed Lindy's systems and processes around safeguarding users' data privacy and security from the ground up. This includes severely limiting access to user data, employing state-of-the-art in-transit and at-rest encryption, and ensuring compliance with relevant legislation such as HIPAA, PIPEDA, and other applicable legislation.
